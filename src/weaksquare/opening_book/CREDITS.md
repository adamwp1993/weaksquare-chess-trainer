# Opening book credits

`a.tsv` to `e.tsv` are the source files of the [Lichess `chess-openings` dataset](https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings), unchanged, at commit `3ba6b20d31b2e6125cb0e6a2e792f400717d9d39`. The dataset is released under the CC0 Public Domain Dedication. A test pins each file's SHA-256.

## Wikibooks

The app ships **no** Wikibooks text. When you open a page, or allow notes to use it, it fetches pages of [Chess Opening Theory](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Chess_Opening_Theory) from English Wikibooks, written by its contributors, under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The app shows that credit with a link to each page wherever its text is used. Test fixtures use invented text.
