## Decisions from Adam (2026-11-08, v0.15 planning)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do your lines survive a restart? | **No.** They're kept while the app is open, across tab switches. No migration. |
| Pasting a PGN or Open in Analysis tab onto a board with moves | **Ask first:** *Replace your current board?* |

## v0.15a: backend (no new dependency, no migration)
- [x] `core/position_setup.py` (no I/O): check a FEN or editor position with python-chess `board.status()`; one clear message per problem (missing or extra king, pawn on the back rank, side not to move in check, too many pieces, castling rights that don't match the pieces, bad en passant square). Never reaches Stockfish; `validate_fen` stays as the second check.
- [x] `core/analysis_pgn.py`: split a pasted PGN with `core/games.parse_pgn`, build the tree with study_pgn's converter. Headers, clocks and `[%…]` commands dropped; comments kept as plain text. A bad PGN raises one error with a message.
- [x] `services/analysis_tab.py`: analysis on the **interactive** engine; popular replies through `OpeningSources`, returning `signed_out` / `offline` cleanly. No LLM dependency (import-graph test), no writes to puzzles, stats, games or Coach tables.
- [x] Bridge methods with `@bridge_call` and the allowlist test updated: `check_board_position`, `import_analysis_pgn`, `analyze_board_position`, `get_popular_replies`.

## Tests (beyond ROADMAP *Done when*)
- Each `status()` problem has a positive and a negative test; a bad FEN or editor position never calls the engine (spy).
- A bad PGN leaves the board unchanged; PGN headers and clocks are absent from what the engine receives; nothing goes to an LLM.
- Row counts and hashes of the puzzle, stats, game and Coach tables are unchanged after a scripted Analysis session.
- Popular replies signed out and offline (fixtures).
- Open in Analysis tab gives the same FEN from a puzzle and from Game Review (e2e).
