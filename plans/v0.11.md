# v0.11 plan: Whole-game coaching

## Context
v0.10 is signed off and merged, and the `v0.11` branch exists and is clean. ROADMAP v0.11 (REQUIREMENTS §10.11, FR-10.51–10.60) extends the Coach beyond tactics so it reads your games the way a tutor would. It adds:
- per-game digests and whole-game measures in the free report
- an open-ended LLM diagnosis that must cite real games and plies
- an opt-in Advanced analysis that also sends SAN move lists
- lesson pointers whose links the app builds
- an opt-in Include my notes

What exploration found:
- Everything the digests need is already stored. `games` has `eco`, `opening`, `pgn`, `time_control` and `extra.division`. Each `game_analysis` row has evals, the best move, MultiPV 3, `phase`, `clock_s`, `low_clock`, themes and habits.
- There's no `weaknesses` table. Weakness kinds live only in `report_json` (`core/coach.py` `Weakness.kind`), and statuses and `weakness_metrics` are v1.0.
- Notes exist only as `mistake_reasons.note`. `CoachReportsRepo.game_reasons()` (`adapters/db/repos/coach.py:166`) returns no notes.
- AI coaching (`core/coach_llm.py`, `services/coach.py:549-640`) is v0.10's key-based schema. `llm_json` holds one answer per report.
- There's no win-chance or accuracy helper in `core`, and no URL builder or host allowlist apart from `services/games.py:502` and `Desktop.open_in_browser` (https only).
- `core/game_explain.py:208 san_moves()` already builds a SAN-only list with no PGN text. Advanced reuses it.
- The anonymization leak list in `tests/integration/test_coach_ai.py` checks for `'"ply"'`. It will be updated, because plies are now cited on purpose.

Branch: `v0.11` (it already exists). Before committing, check `git status` / `git branch` (memory).

## Decisions from Adam (2026-11-04, v0.11 planning)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Minimum sample for whole-game measures | **5 games per group** (opening family, endgame type, …). Below that a measure is shown as *not enough data yet* and never becomes a weakness. "Up +2" means held for a full move (your move and the reply), not a one-ply blip. |
| Curated lesson map | **Lichess Practice + Learn only** (`lichess.org/practice/…`, `lichess.org/learn`). Everything else goes through the search links. |
| AI answers per report | **Keep each answer.** A new table stores every AI run of a report: standard, Advanced and with notes, with the model and cost. A picker switches between them, and findings that are new in a run are labeled. The same options and model reuse the stored answer. |

**My own choices (veto at approval if wrong):**
- **One diagnosis replaces v0.10's AI schema.** Get AI coaching now sends digests plus measures plus v0.10's aggregates and examples. Puzzles-only reports have no games, so they keep the v0.10 prompt.
- **Weaknesses in the AI output:** 3–8, each `{key|null, category, title, explanation, advice, topic|null, evidence[{game:"G3", ply:N}]}`.
  - A `key` must match a measured weakness that was sent. That makes it a tracked weakness; without one it's an observation (FR-10.57).
  - The evidence rule applies to every weakness, keyed ones included.
  - v0.10's `E1…` examples now carry `game` / `ply` labels too, so there's one citation format.
- **Plies are sent 1-based** (half-move numbers) next to the SAN with its move number. The app maps them back to the 0-based `MoveRow.ply`. A cited ply must be a key moment in that game's digest, or, in an Advanced run, any ply of a game whose move list was sent.
- **New weakness kinds and their occurrences** (each needs ≥ 5 games in its group; score = per10 × severity, as in v0.10):
  - `opening:<family>`: you leave the opening (ply 30) at ≤ −100, or make a mistake or blunder in the first 15 moves. The family is the name before `:`, or the ECO code when there's no name.
  - `endgame:<type>`: a mistake or blunder in the endgame phase of that endgame type.
  - `phase:conversion`: a game where you held +2 or better for a full move and didn't win.
  - `time:low_clock`: a mistake or blunder under 10% of your time. It's a weakness only when its error rate is above your error rate the rest of the time.
  - `habit:castledLate`: castling after move 12, or never, while the opponent's queen is on the board. King safety uses the existing habit kind, as FR-10.57 allows.
- **Endgame type:** the material signature (kings excluded) at the first endgame-phase ply: pawn, rook, minor piece, queen or mixed.
- **Pawn structure** at ply 30 (or the opening's end): isolated queen pawn, doubled, isolated and passed pawns, for your side and the opponent's.
- **Trades while ahead or behind:** captures recaptured within 2 plies while the eval is ≥ +150 or ≤ −150.
- **Advanced:**
  - It sends the most recent N games' move lists (the cap: 30 by default, 10–50); every game still gets its digest.
  - There's no new request kind. The detail level's `max_tokens` applies, and the estimate uses the real prompt size.
  - The comparison with a standard run builds both prompts and shows the ratio.
  - "About N minutes" comes from a `TYPICAL_OUTPUT_TOKENS_PER_S` constant in `core/llm/defaults.py`.
  - The Coach request timeout rises to 180 s.
- **Advanced and Include my notes are per AI run, never remembered.** They're off on every open and are never saved in `coach.last_params`. This is how "a checkpoint never uses Advanced" is enforced until v1.0 builds checkpoints: an automatic path can't set them. The full checkpoint test lands in v1.0.
- **Notes:**
  - What's sent: the latest reason and note for your moves in the run's games, plus puzzle and Retry notes in the report's period.
  - URLs are stripped from notes before sending and before the preview (FR-8.16: no URLs, ever).
  - The preview shows the exact strings that will be sent.
- **Lesson drill:** *Learn X* loads that topic's puzzles in Standard mode at −200 from your band. The card says "use Explain after each one". There's no "Explain on" setting, so FR-10.59's wording becomes "Explain encouraged".
- **Lesson links are opened by the backend.** The UI sends only `(topic, target)`. The backend builds the URL, checks the curated host allowlist (`lichess.org`) or the search-site templates, and calls `Desktop.open_in_browser`. Nothing the LLM wrote reaches a URL.
- **Search sites:** YouTube and chess.com are on by default. A custom `https://…{q}…` template is optional. The exact search URL formats are checked with WebFetch while building.
- **Digest size:** 3 key moments per game at Summary and 5 at Detailed, using `key_moments()` (`core/game_review.py:262`).

- v0.12: training plan and journal.
- v2.0: the opening trainer. For an opening weakness, FR-10.58 only lists its games.
