# v0.12 plan: Training plan + journal

## Context
v0.11 is signed off, tagged and merged (`main` = `origin/main` at `72ea65d`, clean). ROADMAP v0.12 (REQUIREMENTS §10.12, FR-10.61–10.73) turns the Coach's diagnosis into a routine and a record:
- a training plan with self-ticking daily checklists, in the Coach and on a Today card
- an AI-proposed plan, checked task by task
- a training journal: your notes, game reflections and an automatic daily log
- **Since last report**, comparing each report with earlier ones
- an ⓘ tooltip on every option

Branch: create `v0.12` from `main`. Save this file as `plans/v0.12.md` once approved, and tick items as they land.

### What exploration found (these shape the design)
- **The existing "Since last report" column breaks the new guard.** `trendText` (`CoachView.tsx:56`) says better/worse with no sample check, and `trend()` (`core/coach.py:299`) treats a new weakness as a rise from 0. FR-10.73 replaces both.
- **Reports don't record their analysis settings.** Think time and thresholds exist only in the digest key (`services/coach.py:601`), so they must be saved from now on. Older reports show "settings unknown".
- **Games-only reports have no puzzle accuracy per theme.** It's computed from `attempts` by time window when read, which also works for older reports.
- **FR-10.68's "no extra tracking" can't fully hold.** `games` has `fetched_at`, but nothing records when a game was analyzed or reviewed. Two small additions are needed, and FR-10.68 gets reworded.
- **An AI answer longer than the limit shows nothing but is still billed** (`services/coach.py:915`), so the limits must rise before the plan goes in.
- **An evidence-only rule would delete "improved" findings.** `validate()` drops any weakness without a game citation, and a weakness that's gone has none. Trend statements need their own validated field.
- **The saved-answer cache would go stale in two ways.** Same-option v0.11 answers have no plan, and a saved answer could be reused after you edit your journal, so the preview would show text that was never sent.
- **Report period vs. journal dates:** reflections are written after the games, so an upper limit of `last_played` would exclude most of them.
- **`App.start` always uses the Mixer's `spec`**, and the Mixer shows its own filters during a session. A plan session needs its own path and a clear on-screen label.
- **No gaps in the closed lists:** `my_severities` already covers "puzzles from your games at a severity level", and `review.show_best` is saved, so a review task must hide best moves without writing that setting.
- **REQUIREMENTS typo:** FR-10.62 cites FR-10.66 for reflections; it should be FR-10.69.

## Decisions from Adam (2026-11-05, v0.12 planning)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which days are training days | **You pick weekdays** when building the plan (default Mon–Fri). Days per week = the number picked. The plan starts today if today is a picked day, otherwise on the next one. |
| Room for the AI plan | **Flat higher limits:** Coach Summary 2,500 → **7,500**, Detailed 5,000 → **10,000** (FR-8.14), for every Coach run. |
| "Stepped through" for a review task | **Every key moment and the final move visited.** |
| Include my journal | **Two checkboxes, one preview** with two sections. Each is off for every run and never saved. |

**Consequences of the flat limits**
- Worst-case estimates rise on every Coach run, so a costly model at High reasoning may now hit the $0.50 per-run cap and be blocked at Confirm (never silently). The self-check lists which seeded models and reasoning levels are affected, and the handoff reports it.
- `TYPICAL_OUTPUT_TOKENS` rises by about 1,400 for a default plan.
- `COACH_TIMEOUT_S` goes from 180 to **300**, because 10,000 tokens at the assumed 50 tokens/s takes 200 s.

**My own choices (veto at approval if wrong)**
- **Your reports only.** Plans, plan progress and the journal apply only to you. An AI run on another player's report asks for no plan and gets no journal.
- **Puzzle-history reports** also get an AI plan (same plan schema, no game tasks), because FR-10.66 covers §10.6.
- **Ticks are worked out from stored events on each read,** not by hooks across services. A task is **done** if you ticked it by hand or the app saw it done. **A hand choice always wins:** a box you unticked stays unticked until you tick it again.
  - Puzzle task: decided attempts in sessions started from that task, added up across sessions. Puzzles solved outside the task don't count.
  - Retry task: every mistake in the game has a `retry_attempts` row since the plan began. Without Stockfish, moves off the stored lines aren't recorded (v0.8-B3), so the hand tick covers it.
  - Review task: see above.
  - Reflection task: an entry saved from that task.
  - **Lesson task (not in FR-10.64, added):** ticks when you open one of its links or start its drill.
- **Minutes spent:** real solving time for puzzle tasks, the task's budgeted minutes for other finished tasks. Labeled "about".
- **Carry-over:** on the next training day after a missed one, its unfinished tasks are added (up to one day's worth); older ones become *missed*. With the setting off, they're dropped. Applied when read, safe to repeat, and testable with the dev time offset.
- **Swap:** deterministic. The next task for the same weakness, otherwise for the next weakness.
- **Puzzle time estimate:** your average from your last 50+ attempts, otherwise 1.5 minutes. Settings: 1–4 weeks, 1–7 weekdays, 10–120 minutes.
- **Plan shape on AI runs:** the AI uses your saved plan settings. A saved answer keeps the shape it was made with, so changing settings never re-bills you.
- **Trend statements** are a new `trend` field: `[{key, change: improved|worse|same, comment}]`. The key must be one sent, and the change must match the app's own guarded result; otherwise it's dropped. This enforces FR-10.73's "checked like any other" and "never inflate".
- **Puzzle accuracy in Since last report:** "then" is your attempts up to the older report, "now" is the attempts between the two. Each side needs at least 10 attempts (`MIN_PUZZLE_ATTEMPTS`). Your report only.
- **Journal in the prompt:**
  - Entries tagged to one of the report's games, plus entries dated from `first_played` onward (no upper limit, like v0.11's notes).
  - Newest 30 entries, each cut to 1,000 characters, with URLs removed. The preview shows the cut text.
  - A game outside the report goes with no game reference.
  - A run stores a hash of the journal text it sent, and a saved answer is reused only if the hash matches.
- **Mistake-reason notes stay out of the journal timeline.** They're still sent through Include my notes.
- **Reflections work on any game in your library.** Another player's report never sends them.
- **Search uses `LIKE`,** with no new FTS index, since a personal journal is small.
- **Today card:** a full-width row at the top of the Puzzles page, only when a plan exists, shrinking to one line while a session runs.
- **Tooltip exemptions:** free-text data entry (PGN paste, search boxes, journal text, keys) gets none. Each exempt control carries `data-no-tip` with a reason, and the Playwright test reads it.
- **No charts.** The five-report trend is text (`2.4 → 1.8 → 1.2`); charts are v1.0's Progress page.

## Out of scope (later versions)
- v1.0: statuses, baselines, checkpoints, the Progress page, and the maintenance share for fixed weaknesses. FR-10.61's share has nothing to act on until statuses exist.
- v2.0: the opening trainer. An opening weakness gets a review task on one of its games.

## Dependencies
**No new PyPI or npm packages.** Mantine already has `Tabs`, `Tooltip` and `Checkbox`.
