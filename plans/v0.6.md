# v0.6 plan: Training modes II + mistake reasons

## Context
v0.5 (Training modes I) was signed off and merged on 2026-11-03, and the board-click fix is on `main`. v0.6 adds:
- **Name the idea first** (FR-4.3): you tag the idea before solving, and your tags are graded against the puzzle's motifs.
- **Danger spotting** (FR-4.4): is the highlighted move safe? About half the candidates are the real blunder and half are moves Stockfish has checked as safe.
- **Mistake reasons** for puzzles (§7.3, FR-5.6–5.11): one-click "why I missed it" chips with keys 1–6, a suggested reason and misclick handling.
- **Stats**: the reason breakdown (FR-9.7) and Commit, Name and Danger accuracy (FR-9.3).

Work happens on branch `v0.6` from `main`. This plan is saved as `plans/v0.6.md` in the first commit and ticked off as items land.

**Requirement IDs in scope:** FR-4.3, FR-4.4, FR-5.6–5.11 (puzzle side), FR-9.3 (the Commit/Name/Danger parts), FR-9.7 (puzzle side), FR-7.2 (safe-candidate checks), the `mistake_reasons` table in §4.

**Out of scope, for later versions:**
- Reasons on Game Review moves and Retry-mode misses, the puzzle-vs-game comparison and the habit-tag filter (v0.8+).
- The Coach's use of reasons (v0.10).
- Explain (v0.7).
- The "Game Review only" option of the ask setting, which arrives in v0.8 with Game Review. v0.6 offers *Always* / *Off*, so there's no dead option.

## Decisions from Adam (planning, 2026-11-03). REQUIREMENTS.md and ROADMAP.md change in the first commit to match
1. **Danger spotting, the refutation is the first move only.** After a correct **Unsafe**, the candidate is played and the board flips to the punishing side. You play one move: the puzzle's first solution move, or any mating move where the solution mates. The rest of the line is shown in the answer.
2. **Danger-spotting results count in Stats and the mistake reasons only.** They never sync to Lichess. They don't put puzzles into Spaced repetition and they don't count for the history filters (*Exclude solved here*, *Only my misses*). FR-4.6 and FR-3.9 say so.
3. **Name the idea, no target square or defender for now.** You tag setup and payoff motifs only, and FR-4.3 is reworded.
4. **Tag score is "found + extras".** Each attempt shows "Ideas found 2 of 3 · 1 extra".
   - Stats shows **ideas found** (named ∩ puzzle ÷ puzzle) and **tags right** (named ∩ puzzle ÷ named).
   - The setup/payoff slot doesn't matter for grading, because Lichess doesn't record which role a theme plays.
   - The graded motifs are the puzzle's themes in the Motifs, Advanced, Mate patterns and Special moves groups.
   - A puzzle with none of those has no tag score and is left out of tag accuracy.
5. **What counts as a safe candidate:**
   - Stockfish analyses the pre-blunder position with MultiPV 5 from the blunderer's side, using your think time.
   - A move is **safe** if it isn't the real blunder, loses **at most 50 cp** against the best line, and doesn't allow a mate.
   - The candidate is picked at random among the safe moves (the best move can be picked).
   - If no move qualifies, that puzzle is shown with the blunder and the next one is owed a safe candidate, so the mix stays about 50/50.
6. **Where a misclick is excluded:** the Stats page, the session bar's accuracy (shown as "1 misclick") and the depth-ladder window. Spaced repetition still schedules the puzzle. It still syncs to Lichess as a loss (FR-5.10).

**My defaults (say if you want otherwise):**
- **Danger spotting needs Stockfish.** If it isn't ready, the mode is still listed, but Start is disabled and the existing install banner shows.
- **Before you make the call**, Hint isn't offered (it would give the answer away) and Show answer is a loss. While you play the refutation, Hint and Show answer work as in Standard.
- **A new suggestion rule for Danger spotting:** calling the real blunder Safe suggests `missedResource`. FR-5.9 gets this line.
- **The Rated toggle is greyed out** in Danger spotting ("stays on this PC").
- **Depth ladder stays Standard / Commit only**, as FR-3.11 already says. The Order option disables it in the new modes, as it does in SRS.
- **Puzzle filters apply** in both new modes, ratios and Random / Easy → hard / Grouped orders included.

## Design (each item gets an ARCHITECTURE §7 entry)

### 1. Storage: migration `user/0005_reasons_modes.sql` (new nullable columns only, decision 4)
- `attempts.idea_tags` (JSON `{"setup":[…],"payoff":[…]}`), plus `idea_found`, `idea_total` and `idea_extra`.
- `attempts.danger_candidate` (UCI), `danger_safe` (1 when the candidate was the safe move) and `danger_call` (`safe`/`unsafe`).
- `mistake_reasons(id PK, source, ref_id, reason, suggested_reason, note, created_at)`, as in REQUIREMENTS §4, with an index on `(source, ref_id)`.
  - `source` is `puzzle` or `danger` in v0.6, and `ref_id` is the attempt id.
  - It's append-only: picking another chip appends a row, and the **latest row per attempt counts**. Skip writes nothing.
- `ModeFields` in `adapters/db/repos/user_data.py` gains the new columns.

### 2. Name the idea first (`core/modes/name_idea.py`)
- `NameIdeaMode` wraps `StandardMode`'s rules (same state plus `tags` and `tagged`).
  - `on_move` raises `ConflictError` until `set_tags(state, setup, payoff)` has been called.
  - Help works as in Standard, and Hint before tagging is allowed (a loss).
- Tags are validated against `IDEA_SETUP` / `IDEA_PAYOFF` (new in `core/themes.py`).
  - **Setup:** the Advanced group, plus sacrifice, capturingDefender and discoveredAttack.
  - **Payoff:** the other Motifs, Mate patterns and Special moves.
  - At least one tag is required, at most 3 per slot.
- Pure `grade_tags(tags, puzzle_themes) -> IdeaScore(found, total, extra)` uses the graded motif set from decision 4. The result is returned with the `Outcome`, after the end (FR-3.14: themes stay hidden until then).
- The angle and the sync are the same as Standard. The mode name is `name_idea`.

### 3. Danger spotting (`core/modes/danger.py`)
- **Safe-candidate picking** (pure): `safe_candidates(lines, blunder, max_loss_cp=50)`.
  - It works on the engine's `AnalysisLine`s (mover's side) and drops the blunder, any mate against the mover, and any move more than 50 cp worse than the best line (mate scores mapped to large cp values).
  - `pick_candidate(rng, owed_safe, safe_list, blunder)` returns the candidate and whether it's the safe one.
- **`DangerMode` state:** the puzzle, the pre-blunder board (raw FEN), the candidate, `is_safe`, the phase (`call` → `refute` → `done`), and the analysis kept for after the end.
  - `on_call(state, call, now)`:
    - Safe/Safe → win.
    - Unsafe on a safe move → loss.
    - Safe on the blunder → loss.
    - Unsafe on the blunder → push `moves[0]` and go to the `refute` phase.
  - `on_move` in `refute`: `is_acceptable(board, move, moves[1])` → win, else a loss. Either way the puzzle ends.
  - `first_move_correct` = the call was right, and `line_correct` = win.
- **Session** (`services/session.py`): the mode registry gains `name_idea` and `danger_spotting`.
  - For Danger, `next_puzzle` flips a coin with the injected `rng` (or uses the owed safe slot).
  - For a safe slot it runs `validate_fen` → `EngineService.analyse(board, blunderer, multipv=5)` on the **interactive engine** with key `(session, "danger")`.
  - It raises `EngineNotInstalled` if there's no engine. The UI shows "Preparing the position…" for the roughly 1 s this takes.
  - `PuzzleView` gains `candidate`. In Danger mode `opponent_move` is empty, so the real blunder **never reaches the front end** before the end (a test pins this).
- **After the end:**
  - Blunder: "Why it's unsafe" plays the puzzle line from `moves[1]` (no engine needed).
  - Safe: "Stockfish: +0.3 for you after Nf3 (best +0.4)", using the stored analysis.
  - The engine panel and Analyze work as usual (FR-7.3 is unchanged: nothing before the end).
- **Local only:** `_record` sends no sync fields for Danger (`sync_status 'local'`). `srs_rows`, `SOLVED_IDS` / `LATEST_MISS` (puzzles.py) and `played_before` exclude `mode = 'danger_spotting'`.

### 4. Mistake reasons (`core/reasons.py`, pure)
- `REASONS` holds the 6 keys, labels and gap types in FR-5.6 order, so keys 1–6 follow that order.
- `suggest(ctx) -> reason | None`. `ctx` carries the mode, the decisive move's think time, whether the first move was right and a later one wrong, the help used, the refutation's first reply and score, the idea score and the Danger call. The first rule that matches wins, in this order:
  1. `rushed`: the deciding move (or Submit, or the Danger call) came less than `reasons.rushed_s` after its position appeared (default 3 s, range 1–30).
  2. `miscalculated`: the first move was right and a later player move was wrong (Standard, SRS, Name, Commit).
  3. `missedResource`: the refutation's first reply is a check or a capture, or its score is a mate against you; or, in Danger spotting, you called the real blunder Safe.
  4. `unknownPattern`: in Name the idea, none of your tags was a puzzle motif (and the puzzle has motifs).
- The modes record per-move timing: `StandardState`/`CommitState` gain `position_ms` (when the current position appeared).
- **Service** (`services/reasons.py`):
  - `prompt(session_id)` returns the attempt id, the source and the suggestion. It uses the cached refutation if there is one, or fetches it when an engine is ready. With no engine, `missedResource` isn't suggested.
  - `set_reason(attempt_id, reason, note)` checks that the attempt exists and is a loss, the reason key is valid, and the note is at most 200 characters (stored as plain text). It uses the latest suggestion.
- **Misclick exclusion (decision 6):**
  - `StatsRepo` left-joins the latest reason. `core/stats.py` drops misclick attempts from every tally and adds `misclicks`.
  - There's a warning when misclicks are at least 25% of tagged misses, with at least 8 tagged.
  - `session_tally` excludes misclicks and returns their count.
  - `ladder_history` excludes them.
  - The sync never looks at reasons (a test pins that a misclick is still sent with `win: false`).

### 5. Bridge, settings, Stats
- **New bridge methods** (the allowlist test is updated in the same commit):
  - `set_idea_tags(session_id, setup, payoff)`
  - `call_danger(session_id, call)`
  - `get_reason_prompt(session_id)`
  - `set_mistake_reason(attempt_id, reason, note)`
  - `get_reason_stats(theme, mode)`
- `start_session` accepts the two new modes.
- New settings through validators: `reasons.ask` (`always`/`off`, default `always`) and `reasons.rushed_s`.
- `dto.py`/`types.ts` regenerated (`scripts/gen_types.py`), and `mockClient`/`mockEngine` implement everything.
- **Stats (FR-9.3, 9.7):**
  - `MODE_LABELS` gains the two modes.
  - A **Mode details** card: Commit calculation accuracy (Σ replies_correct ÷ Σ replies_total), Name *ideas found* / *tags right*, and Danger accuracy overall and split by safe candidates vs blunders, so a bias toward "Unsafe" shows.
  - A **Why you missed** section: a breakdown table (reason, count, share, gap type) with Theme and Mode filters, a weekly trend (stacked bars), misclicks on their own line and the high-misclick notice.

### 6. Front end
- `state/modes.ts` gets the two new modes. `MixerView`:
  - In Danger: Start is disabled with the engine banner when Stockfish isn't ready, and the Rated toggle is greyed out.
  - Depth ladder is disabled in the new modes.
- `TrainerView`:
  - **Name the idea:** a `components/IdeaTags.tsx` panel (Setup / Payoff multi-selects, **Start solving**). The board is read-only until you press it. The result panel shows the tag score with each tag marked ✓/✗ and the motifs you missed.
  - **Danger spotting:** a `components/DangerCall.tsx` panel with an arrow on the candidate and **Safe** / **Unsafe** buttons (keys `S`/`U`). After Unsafe, the board flips to the punishing side with "Punish it: find the refutation". The result explains why the move was safe or unsafe.
- **`components/ReasonChips.tsx`:**
  - Shown on the result panel after a loss (not practice runs, and only with `reasons.ask = always`).
  - 6 chips, numbered 1–6, with the suggested one highlighted ("Suggested") but saved only on click.
  - "Add a note" (optional), Skip, and changeable until you move on. It never blocks Next.
- Shortcuts 1–6, `S` and `U` are added to `state/shortcuts.ts` and `ShortcutsHelp`.
- Settings → Training gets "Ask why I missed: Always / Off" and "Rushed if under N s".
- `SessionBar` shows misclicks.
- `StatsView` gets the new card and section.
