# v0.8 plan: Game library + Game Review (engine)

## Context
v0.7 was signed off and merged on 2026-11-03. v0.8 adds your own games, in five parts (ROADMAP v0.8):
- **Linked accounts and the library:** a linked chess.com username with **Verify**, plus a **My games** library that syncs both platforms incrementally (§11.5).
- **One-off import:** a pasted PGN, a `.pgn` file, a Lichess URL or a chess.com URL (§11.1).
- **Engine analysis and Review:** a local Stockfish pass over every position on a new **background engine**, stored in `game_analysis`, then a **Review** screen with an eval graph, move symbols, key moments and refutations (§11.2–11.3).
- **Learning from your games:** **Retry mode** on your mistakes, **Save as puzzle**, and mistake reasons on game moves (FR-11.15, 11.16, FR-5.7).
- **Puzzle files:** import your own puzzle files (PGN / EPD / CSV) into `custom_puzzles` and drill them through the new **My puzzles** source (FR-10.46–10.50, FR-3.15).

Work happens on branch `v0.8`, created from `main` (check the branch first, per memory). This plan is saved as `plans/v0.8.md` in the first commit and ticked off as items land.

**Requirement IDs in scope:**
- **Game Review:** FR-11.1–11.16, FR-11.22–11.29, FR-11.31–11.33. FR-11.10 counts only partly (decision 2).
- **Puzzles and the source:** FR-3.15, FR-10.46–10.50, FR-10.43's tolerance rule (decision 3).
- **Mistake reasons:** FR-5.7 and FR-5.8 (games and retry), and FR-5.9's game rules.
- **Stats:** FR-9.7's source filter (puzzles vs games).
- **Schema (§4.3):** `games`, `account_sync`, `game_analysis`, `custom_puzzles`.

**Out of scope (later versions):**
- **v0.9:** Explain this move, Explain my game, *Review + Explain my game*, and the AI-reviewed status.
- **v0.10:**
  - Send to Coach, the lichess-puzzler tagger and theme tags on mistakes or puzzles, and habit tags.
  - Coach-made puzzles: the severity setting, the quality check, Missed/Allowed puzzles solved as your opponent, and archive.
  - The `game_errors` view, which is created with its first reader in v0.10.
- **Also out:** Chess960 and other variants (rejected), and any shipped or downloaded puzzle collection.

## Decisions from Adam (planning, 2026-11-03). REQUIREMENTS.md and ROADMAP.md change in the first commit to match
1. **Every game is analyzed locally.** Our Stockfish runs on every game, including Lichess games already analyzed there, so every review is graded the same way. Lichess's accuracy % and an *Analyzed on Lichess* badge still show. FR-11.3 is reworded.
2. **No theme tags in v0.8.** Each user mistake is classified Missed / Allowed / Other, with no themes. Saved and imported puzzles stay untagged until v0.10's tagger tags them. FR-11.10 gets a "themes from v0.10" note.
3. **Saved-from-game puzzles use the 30 cp tolerance with the engine.** Any move within 30 cp of the best counts (configurable, shared with Retry mode). The interactive engine checks it and then plays the reply. This builds FR-10.43's solving rule now, and v0.10 reuses it. **Imported file puzzles are strict** (FR-10.49).
4. **The source is called "My puzzles"** (Lichess DB / My puzzles / Both). It holds game puzzles and imported sets. FR-3.15 and FR-10.47 are reworded.

**My defaults (say if you want otherwise):**
- **Custom puzzle position:** `fen` is the position to solve, and the side to move is you. There's no opponent pre-move to animate, unlike Lichess puzzles (§4.2).
  - Game puzzles store the line from your mistake position: the best move, then the engine's PV, cut to whole player moves and at most 3 player moves.
  - Imported puzzles store the file's line, cut to end on your move.
- **Modes for My puzzles:** Standard, Commit the line and Spaced repetition. Name the idea and Danger spotting stay Lichess-only for now: Name the idea has nothing to grade until puzzles are tagged, and Danger spotting needs a blunder.
  - In those two modes the source control is locked to Lichess DB, with a tooltip.
  - **In Commit the line,** game puzzles are graded against the stored line, with any mate accepted, because tolerance grading needs a played line.
- **Filters with My puzzles:**
  - **Lichess-only:** themes, rating, openings, quality, played on Lichess, ratios and the depth-ladder order. They're greyed out for My puzzles.
  - **Shared:** number of moves, side to move, exclude solved here and only my misses.
  - **New My-puzzles filters:** origin (from my games / imported), set label, Missed / Allowed, phase and time control. Severity waits for v0.10.
  - **Both:** a random mix proportional to the two pool sizes.
- **Never synced:** `my:` attempts are always stored `local`, with no rated badge.
- **Without Stockfish,** game puzzles fall back to strict checking on the stored line, with a note.
- **Game moves, before analysis (FR-11.8):** 0.3 s per position, MultiPV 3, on a second Stockfish process.
  - The background engine gets `max(1, threads − 1)` threads, so the interactive engine keeps a core.
  - Analysis is reused when the game, the think time and the engine ID all match (FR-11.31). A different setting re-analyzes only when you ask.
- **Classification (FR-11.9, configurable):**
  - Thresholds: inaccuracy ≥ 50, mistake ≥ 100, blunder ≥ 200 cp or a mate swing.
  - Positions beyond ±500 cp before the move aren't penalized.
  - Evals are clamped to ±1000 for cp loss.
- **Missed / Allowed / Other (FR-10.11, simple rule, refined in v0.10):**
  - **Missed:** your best move kept you at least +150 and you dropped below it by the mistake threshold.
  - **Allowed:** otherwise, when the opponent's best reply after your move is at least +150 for them, or mates.
  - **Other:** everything else.
- **Phase:** from Lichess's `division` when it's there. Otherwise a rule:
  - **Opening:** moves 1–10 while there are at least 13 non-pawn pieces.
  - **Endgame:** 6 or fewer non-pawn, non-king pieces.
  - **Middlegame:** everything else.
- **Low clock:** under 10% of the starting time, from `%clk` comments when they exist.
- **Key moments:** your 5 largest cp losses that are at least inaccuracies.
- **Refutation at a move:** the stored PV of the next position, so the Review screen needs no live engine call.
- **Retry results:** a new table, `retry_attempts`. They show on Stats as *Retry: found N of M*.
  - Reasons use `source = 'game'` with `ref_id = "<game_id>:<ply>"`, and `'retry'` with `ref_id = <retry_attempts.id>`.
  - The ask setting gains **Game Review only**.
- **Lichess library sync** needs you to be signed in (FR-11.23). Lichess URL import works without signing in, as a public export.
- **chess.com URL import** searches your linked username's archives newest-first, at most the last 6 months, one request at a time. If you have no linked username, it asks for it once. If the game isn't found, it asks you to paste the PGN (FR-11.4).
- **First sync (FR-11.27):** the last 90 days or 200 games per account, whichever is smaller. Sync on open is on by default.
- **Limits:**
  - A game file can be at most 10 MB and is listed up to 500 games.
  - A puzzle file can be at most 20 MB and 20,000 entries.
  - A game can be at most 400 plies.

## Design (each item gets an ARCHITECTURE §7 entry)

### 1. Storage: migration `user/0007_games.sql` (new tables only)
- **`games`:** as in §4.3, plus these columns:
  - `moves_hash`: SHA-256 of the start FEN and the UCI moves, used for dedupe on paste and file imports.
  - `extra_json`: Lichess's division and accuracy.
  - `lichess_analyzed`.
  - `analysis_think_s` and `analysis_engine`.
  - Constraints: `UNIQUE(source_platform, source_game_id)`.
- **`account_sync(platform PK, username, last_sync_at, last_archive, etag_json)`**.
- **`game_analysis`:** as in §4.3, keyed by `(game_id, ply)`, with these columns:
  - `fen_before`, `move_uci`, `move_san`, `mover`
  - `eval_before` and `eval_after` (cp and mate as JSON, from the mover's view)
  - `best_move`, `multipv_json`, `cp_loss`, `classification`, `kind`, `phase`, `low_clock`, `clock_s`
  - `themes`, which stays NULL until v0.10.
- **`custom_puzzles`:** as in §4.3, plus `set_label`, `name`, `strict` and `opponent_reply_engine`.
  - `origin` is manual / import (coach comes in v0.10).
  - Unique indexes: partial `UNIQUE(game_id, ply) WHERE game_id IS NOT NULL`, and `UNIQUE(set_label, fen, solution_uci) WHERE origin='import'`.
- **`retry_attempts(id, game_id, ply, played_uci, found, cp_loss, created_at)`**.
- **New `settings` keys:**
  - `chesscom.username`, `games.sync_on_open`, `games.first_sync_days`, `games.first_sync_max`
  - `review.think_s`, `review.thresholds`, `review.tolerance_cp`, `review.key_moments`
  - `reasons.ask` gains `game_review`.

### 2. Core (pure; unit-tested)
- **`core/games.py`:** the `GameSource` protocol, plus these pieces:
  - The `GameRecord` and `ParsedGame` types.
  - `parse_pgn(text) -> list[ParsedGame | GameError]`. It handles multiple games and rejects variants (the `Variant` header, or `SetUp` with a non-standard start), illegal moves and empty games, with a clear reason for each.
  - `detect_color(headers, usernames)`, `moves_hash` and `parse_clocks`.
  - `time_class(time_control)`, used when chess.com leaves it out.
  - `parse_lichess_url` and `parse_chesscom_url`.
- **`core/chesscom.py`:** `parse_archive_game(dict) -> GameRecord | Skip`.
  - Only the documented core fields are required; everything else is optional (FR-11.28).
  - Games whose `rules != "chess"` are skipped.
- **`core/game_review.py`:**
  - `classify(eval_before, eval_after, thresholds)` and `cp_loss` with the decided-position rule.
  - `missed_or_allowed`, `phase_of` and `key_moments`.
  - `side_summary`: the counts, the average cp loss, the breakdown by phase, low-clock mistakes and the platform accuracy.
- **`core/tolerance.py`:** `within_tolerance(best_score, played_score, tol_cp)`, shared by Retry mode and game puzzles.
- **`core/puzzle_files.py`:** `parse_pgn_puzzles`, `parse_epd` (`bm`/`pv` in SAN) and `parse_csv` (`fen,solution[,name]`, UCI or SAN, read by header).
  - Each returns `ImportedPuzzle` items or `EntryError(n, reason)`.
  - Each validates the FEN and every move with python-chess and cuts the line to end on the solver's move.
- **`core/puzzle.py`:** `SOURCES` gains `"my"`.
- **`core/filters.py`:**
  - `source` validation now allows `my` and `both`.
  - New fields: `my_origin`, `my_sets`, `my_kind`, `my_phase` and `my_time_class`.
  - `SAVED_FILTERS_VERSION` 3 → 4, with an `upgrade_saved` step.
- **`core/reasons.py`:**
  - `Source` gains `game` and `retry`.
  - Game suggestion rules:
    - `rushed` on a low clock
    - `miscalculated` when the best move's first move was played but the line failed (only possible in Retry)
    - `missedResource` when the refutation's first reply is a check, capture or mate

### 3. Adapters
- **`adapters/http.py`:**
  - `get_json_cached(url, etag) -> (status, data, etag)` for ETag/304 support.
  - An optional `accept=` for the JSON export of a single Lichess game.
- **`adapters/lichess_games.py` (`LichessGames`):**
  - `GET /api/games/user/{u}` as ndjson with `since`, `max`, `pgnInJson`, `clocks`, `evals`, `accuracy` and `opening`, using the token.
  - `GET /game/export/{id}` as JSON with the same flags plus `division`.
- **`adapters/chesscom.py` (`ChesscomGames`):**
  - Endpoints: `GET /pub/player/{u}` (Verify), `/pub/player/{u}/games/archives` and each monthly archive.
  - A **lock serializes every request**, enforcing the one-at-a-time rule.
  - It uses ETags, treats 410 as permanent and backs off on 429.
  - It sends the app's User-Agent with the repo URL.
- **`adapters/stockfish.py`:** no new methods. `EngineService` owns a second instance, `background`, with its own lock and ticket-free calls (ARCHITECTURE line 409).
- **`adapters/desktop.py`:** `choose_pgn_file()` and `choose_puzzle_import_file()`. They're file dialogs, and the backend reads the file.
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/fakes/games.py`: `FakeLichessGames` and `FakeChesscom`. The chess.com fake records concurrency, so the serial rule can be asserted.
  - `tests/fixtures/http/chesscom/` and `http/lichess_games/`: hand-written from the API docs and **anonymized**: `player_*`/`opponent_*`, `GAMEID…` IDs, no real URLs.
  - The scrub test is extended to chess.com game URLs.

### 4. Services
- **`services/engine.py`:**
  - `analyse_background(board, think_s, multipv)` runs on the second process, which is started lazily and closed on shutdown.
  - The interactive path is unchanged.
- **`services/games.py` (`GameLibraryService`):**
  - **Accounts:** `link_chesscom(username)` with Verify (shows the profile, or *no such user* on a 404) and `unlink`.
  - **Sync:** `start_sync()` runs the `games_sync` job for both platforms. It's incremental through `account_sync`, uses the first-sync window, stores metadata and PGNs only, and runs on open when that's on.
  - **Library:** `list_games(filter)` with the FR-11.26 filters and paging.
  - **Import:** `import_pgn(text)` and `import_file(path)` (returns the list when there are several games) and `import_url(url)`.
  - **Your color:** detected from your configured usernames. Otherwise the import asks.
  - **Dedupe:** by source ID or `moves_hash` (FR-11.7).
- **`services/analysis.py` (`AnalysisService`):**
  - `queue(game_ids)` adds games to an in-memory FIFO queue drained by one `game_analysis` job at a time (FR-11.31), with progress per game and per position, and cancel.
  - Every FEN is validated before it's sent.
  - It skips a game that's already analyzed at the same settings.
  - It writes `game_analysis` in one transaction per game and sets `analysis_status`.
- **`services/review.py` (`ReviewService`):**
  - `get_review(game_id)` returns the moves, symbols, evals for the graph, the summary for each side, the key moments with each move's lines and refutation, and the reasons.
  - **Retry:** `retry_move(game_id, ply, uci)` validates the move, scores it with the interactive engine, checks it with `within_tolerance`, records a `retry_attempts` row and returns found/not found plus the answer. The answer is never sent before you try.
  - **Save:** `save_as_puzzle(game_id, ply)` creates a `custom_puzzles` row with `origin = manual`, and returns *already saved* when the row exists.
  - `set_game_reason`.
- **`services/puzzle_import.py`:** `start_import(path, label)` is a job with progress and cancel. It writes the report (entry number and reason) and returns the counts: added, duplicates and skipped (FR-10.48).
- **`adapters/db/repos/custom_puzzles.py` (`CustomPuzzleSource`):** implements `PuzzleSource` for `my:` with an integer key space.
- **`services/session.py`:**
  - **Queue:** draws from the Lichess source, `CustomPuzzleSource`, or both, with the pool tagged per key.
  - **Loading a `my:` puzzle:** no pre-move.
  - **Moves on tolerance puzzles:** a move that matches the stored line is instant. Anything else goes to the interactive engine. If it's accepted, the engine plays the reply and continues the line for the remaining player moves.
  - **`_record`:** already marks non-Lichess attempts as local.
- **Repos that are Lichess-only today and now handle `my:`:**
  - `srs_rows` and the SRS key map
  - `SOLVED_IDS` and `MISSED_IDS`
  - `LATEST_REASON`, which now covers `game` and `retry` for the reason stats only. The ladder and puzzle accuracy still read `puzzle` and `danger` only.
  - The `stats` join: a `my:` puzzle's length comes from `custom_puzzles`.
- **`services/reasons.py`:** game and retry refs. `get_reason_stats` gains `source = puzzles | games | all` (FR-9.7).

### 5. Bridge (allowlist test and `gen_types.py` updated in the same commit)
New `@bridge_call` methods:
- **Accounts:** `verify_chesscom(username)`, `set_chesscom_username(username | None)`.
- **Library:**
  - `start_games_sync()` and `list_games(filter)`
  - `import_game_pgn(text)`, `choose_and_import_game_file()`, `import_game_url(url)` and `pick_imported_game(token, index, color)`
  - `set_game_color(game_id, color)` and `open_game_online(game_id)`
- **Analysis:** `analyze_games(ids)` and `get_analysis_queue()`.
- **Review:** `get_review(game_id)`, `retry_move(game_id, ply, uci)`, `save_game_puzzle(game_id, ply)` and `set_game_reason(game_id, ply, reason, note)`.
- **Puzzle files:** `choose_and_import_puzzles(label)`, `get_puzzle_import_report(job_id)` and `list_puzzle_sets()`.
- **Counts:** `count_matches` already takes a `FilterSpec`, so it counts My puzzles as well.

### 6. Front end
- **App:** `page` state (`trainer | games | review`). The header gets **My games** and **Import game** buttons. Pages stay React state, with no router.
- **My games page:**
  - A Mantine table with the FR-11.25 columns and status badges.
  - The FR-11.26 filters.
  - **Refresh**, with sync progress that doesn't block.
  - Multi-select **Analyze selected**.
  - The analysis queue's progress, with cancel.
  - Row actions: **Review** and **Open on Lichess / chess.com**.
- **Import game dialog:**
  - Inputs: paste, file (pick from a list when it holds several games), or URL.
  - It asks for your color when it can't detect it, with *Review both sides* as a choice.
  - Clear errors, including *variants aren't supported*.
- **Review page:**
  - **Board and moves:** the `Board`, and a move list with ?! / ? / ?? symbols where your moves are highlighted.
  - **Eval graph:** a `recharts` area chart, clamped to ±10. Click it to jump to a move.
  - **Summary:** one per side.
  - **Key moments:** *Played X, better was Y*, the swing, `ReasonChips` and **Save as puzzle**.
  - **At any move:** the best-move arrow, the top 3 lines (stored data), and the refutation, which you step through with `AnswerPlayer` segments.
  - **Retry mode:** walk through your mistakes, play a move, see whether it was found, then the answer. A miss offers the reason chips.
  - An analysis progress bar shows when the game isn't analyzed yet.
- **Mixer:**
  - `SourceSelect` (Lichess DB / My puzzles / Both), and a **My puzzles** filter group.
  - Lichess-only controls are greyed out with a tooltip.
  - The collapsed summary names the source.
- **Import puzzles dialog:** in the ☰ menu (*Import puzzles from a file…*). It takes a set label and a file, shows progress and cancel, and then shows the report table.
- **Settings:**
  - A new **Games** section: the chess.com username with Verify and the profile, sync on open, the first-sync window, the analysis think time, the thresholds, the retry tolerance and the number of key moments.
  - The reasons ask setting gets **Game Review only**.
- **Stats:** a source filter on the reason breakdown, a *Retry: found N of M* card, and accuracy per source.
- **Mock client:** a fixed library (Lichess and chess.com games, analyzed and not), one fully analyzed review, a multi-game file, a puzzle-import report with skipped entries, and a My-puzzles pool.
